IMSEC Ghaziabad

Located in the industrial city of Ghaziabad, IMS Engineering College is known for quality technical and management education, centres of excellence, innovative teaching pedagogy, discipline, and other creative efforts.

IMSEC Ghaziabad is one of the top-notch Engineering colleges in North India which has created a niche for itself since its establishment in 2002. It is ranked 4th in North zone among the top Private Engineering colleges by Times Engineering Institute Ranking Survey 2024.

Mr. Satyam Pratap, Ms Roli Singh, Ms Haritima Chaturvedi, Mr. Shubham Chaudhary & Mr. Gaurav Singh students of final year (Electrical & Electronics Engineering) showcased their prototype model MARS ROVER at Edinburgh University, United Kingdom during 10-17 February 2019.
